Privacy policy

  • Applies to: the Timeline Visualizer mobile app (Android and iOS) and this website
  • Effective date: 21 August 2026
  • Developer: MahlerLab
  • Contact: mahlerlabdiy@gmail.com

In short. Timeline Visualizer is built to process sensitive location history on your device. Your Timeline file, your route, your titles, and your videos are never uploaded to the developer. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no developer-operated server. The one thing that leaves your phone is a request for map tiles — and the app asks before the first time.

1. Data the app can access

The app reads only the files you explicitly choose in your device's system file picker: the Google Maps Timeline export (Timeline.json) and any MP4 videos you add to the library. It does not request device location, Google account access, contacts, your photo library for browsing, advertising identifiers, or broad storage permission.

The information it works with locally is:

  • the location points, timestamps, and journeys inside the Timeline file you selected;
  • the name and title template you type for video titles;
  • your video settings — period, length, camera, map style, colours, orientation, and quality;
  • the videos you create or add, with their thumbnails and details.

2. Data stored on your device

  • The Timeline file. The file picker hands the app its own copy of the file you choose, which is kept in the app's private storage so the same Timeline can be reused for your next video. Choosing a different file replaces it. It is never uploaded.
  • Export data in progress. While a video is being created, the selected route points and export settings are held in private app storage so the work can continue when the app is not on screen and can resume if the system restarts the app. This data is removed after completion, cancellation, or failure.
  • Finished videos. Videos are written to app storage and then published to your gallery — on Android under Movies/Timeline Visualizer, on iOS to your photo library. Save as writes an additional copy wherever you choose.
  • Video library index. A local index holds each video's location, title, filename, length, creation date, and Timeline period, plus a small thumbnail in private storage. On Android these are excluded from backup and device transfer, so they are not copied to another device.
  • Overview images. A 1080 × 1080 route overview may sit in the app's cache for the completion screen. No visible image file is created unless you choose to save it.
  • Preferences. Your defaults — title template, name, period, camera and advanced settings, colours, quality, orientation, and theme — are stored in the app's own settings storage.
  • Map tile cache. Downloaded basemap images are cached temporarily to reduce network use, and are cleared when you clear the app's cache or uninstall the app.

3. Network use

Map tiles from CARTO

To draw the map, the app requests raster map tiles from CARTO. Those requests contain standard zoom/x/y tile identifiers plus normal network metadata such as your IP address and user agent. Tile identifiers correspond to the geographic areas in your selected Timeline and may therefore reveal those areas to CARTO. Before your first Timeline is loaded, the app explains this transfer and lets you cancel.

Elevation tiles from AWS

When the 3D + terrain map style is selected, the app additionally requests elevation tiles from the AWS Open Data terrain set. These carry the same kind of zoom/x/y identifiers, sampled several zoom levels coarser than the basemap, so they identify the same areas less precisely. No elevation request is made for the flat or plain 3D styles.

What is never sent

The Timeline JSON, your route coordinates, video frames, titles, entered name, and generated videos are not sent to CARTO, to AWS, or to the developer. All network requests use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Checking for updates or opening the project page launches your browser at a page you asked for; the destination site then applies its own privacy practices.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is used
InternetDownloading map tiles, and elevation tiles for the terrain style.
Notifications (Android 13+)Showing video progress and a completion alert. Declining does not stop video creation and grants no access to personal data.
Foreground service (Android)Continuing a video export you started after the app leaves the screen, with a visible progress notification and a Cancel action.
Photo library access (iOS)Saving a finished video to your photo library. Only videos this app creates or that you add are written.

The app does not request location permission — it reads history from your exported file, not from your device's GPS.

5. No accounts, analytics, or advertising

Every feature works without signing in. The app has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, tracking identifiers, in-app purchases, or developer-operated server. Because nothing is uploaded to the developer, there is no account data for the developer to disclose, correct, or delete on request.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data from children.

7. Deleting your data

  • Cached map tiles. Android: Settings → Apps → Timeline Visualizer → Storage & cache → Clear cache. iOS: offload or delete the app.
  • Everything the app stores. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it. That removes the copied Timeline file, preferences, the video index, and thumbnails.
  • A video file. Removing an entry from My videos does not delete the MP4. Use the confirmed Delete video action, or delete the file from where it is stored.
  • Your Google Timeline data itself. That lives in your Google account and is managed in Google Maps, not in this app.

8. Third-party services

Map tiles come from CARTO and use OpenStreetMap data. Elevation tiles for the terrain style come from the AWS Open Data terrain set, built from public-domain surveys. Their own terms and privacy practices apply to those requests:

  • CARTO privacy notice
  • OpenStreetMap privacy policy
  • AWS privacy notice
  • Terrain Tiles on AWS Open Data

App distribution is handled by Google Play and the Apple App Store, which collect their own installation and account information under their respective policies.

9. This website

These pages are static. They set no cookies, run no analytics, and ask for no personal information. If the site is hosted by a third party such as GitHub Pages, that host may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, under its own policy.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles data changes, this page and the copy shipped with the app are updated and the effective date above is revised. Material changes are also noted in the release notes.

11. Contact
