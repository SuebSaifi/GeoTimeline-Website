Features

Everything Timeline Visualizer 2.1.3 can do, from choosing a period to the finished MP4.

Choose what goes in the video

📄

Your own Timeline export

Reads the Timeline.json file exported from Android or iPhone. The most recent file is remembered, so the next video starts without choosing again.

📅

Exact dates or month ranges

Pick a start and end date, or an inclusive month range that can span several years — a single weekend or a whole decade.

✏️

Reusable title template

Write a template such as {year} {name}'s Timeline once. The year and name fill themselves in for every video you make afterwards.

📊

Period summary before you commit

Each selection shows its point count, approximate distance, and date span, so you know whether the period actually contains a journey.

Shape how the video looks

🎥

Camera movement

Fixed holds the whole journey in frame, Steady drifts gently along the route, and Dynamic follows closely and zooms into detail.

🧭

Map style and perspective

Light or dark basemap, drawn Flat, in 3D, or as 3D + terrain with real ground heights under the route.

🎨

Route and marker colours

Seven presets from rose to amber, or a custom colour chosen by hue, shade, or hex value — set separately for the route line and the moving marker.

🖼️

Orientation and quality

Square (1:1), portrait (9:16), or landscape (16:9), rendered at 480p, 720p, or 1080p — sized for the place you plan to post it.

Advanced camera and timing controls

Long journeys and short city trips need different pacing. The advanced settings let you tune the camera without touching a timeline editor, and every choice is saved as the default for your next video.

SettingChoicesWhat it changes
Camera movement Fixed · Steady · Dynamic How much the camera follows the route instead of holding a wide view.
Route context Compact 2% · Balanced 5% · Generous 8% · Maximum 10% How much surrounding map stays visible around the route.
Local framing Detailed · Balanced · Wide How tightly the camera frames the part of the journey being drawn.
Zoom-in smoothness Quick · Gentle · Cinematic How gradually the camera closes in when the route slows down.
Long-hop sensitivity More sensitive · Automatic · Less sensitive When a big jump — a flight, say — counts as a hop rather than travel.
Long-trip compression Off 1.00 · Gentle 0.92 · Balanced 0.85 · Strong 0.75 Shortens the time spent on very long legs so short trips still get screen time.
Route animation length Presets, or any whole number from 10 to 300 seconds The length of the animated portion of the video.
Video quality Standard 480p · High 720p · Ultra 1080p Output resolution, rendering time, and file size.

A single Restore defaults action puts every advanced setting back if a change did not work out.

Accurate routes from messy GPS

🧹

Outlier filtering

Isolated, physically impossible points are ignored conservatively, and the app tells you how many it skipped. Filtering can be turned off if you would rather see everything.

⚡

One rendering path

The preview and the exported video are drawn by the same code, so the finished MP4 looks like what you previewed.

🔁

Live preview

Scrub the animation, pause it, and change the period or colours — the preview rebuilds without exporting anything.

After the render

🔔

Background export

On Android, encoding continues after you leave the app, with a progress notification, a Cancel action, and an alert when the video is ready.

🎞️

Video library

Finished videos appear in My videos with a thumbnail, title, date, length, and the period they cover. Older MP4 files can be added to the same list.

🖨️

Journey overview image

Every finished video comes with a 1080 × 1080 overview of the complete route that you can save or share as a PNG.

📤

Play, share, save a copy

Watch videos inside the app, share them anywhere, save a copy to another location, or delete one — or the whole library — with confirmation.

Around the app

  • Simple navigation. My videos, New video, and Settings sit in the bottom bar.
  • Saved defaults. Settings hold the defaults used by every new preview and export.
  • Appearance. Match the system theme, or force light or dark — your videos are unaffected.
  • Nine languages. English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.
  • Restoration guide. Built-in instructions for importing an older encrypted Timeline backup into Google Maps before exporting.
  • No account. Every feature works without signing in, and there are no ads or in-app purchases.
